# Self-Assessment & Decision-Making Frameworks

A curated overview of established psychological, career, cognitive, and personal growth frameworks available for free, anonymous browser-based testing on [Tester.lt](https://www.tester.lt/en/).

## Why Privacy-First Assessments Matter
Most online personality and career test platforms use dark patterns - allowing users to spend 15 minutes answering questions only to lock the final evaluation behind a paywall or mandatory email registration. 

[Tester.lt](https://www.tester.lt/en/) provides a clean, transparent alternative:
- **100% Free & Anonymous:** Instant scoring directly in the browser with zero sign-ups or email walls.
- **Science-Backed & Practical:** Grounded in established psychological and coaching frameworks.
- **AI-Enhanced Insights:** Optional AI-powered deep dives for key assessments (including Big Five OCEAN, Life Balance Audit, and Cartesian Decision Square).
- **Bilingual Architecture:** Full library available in both [English](https://www.tester.lt/en/) and [Lithuanian](https://www.tester.lt/).

## Core Frameworks & Interactive Tools (Direct Links)

### 1. Personality & Cognitive Styles
- **[Big Five (OCEAN) Personality Test](https://www.tester.lt/en/tests/big-five/):** Measures Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Neuroticism. Includes optional AI-generated personalized analysis.
- **[VARK Learning Styles Assessment](https://www.tester.lt/en/tests/learning-style/):** Evaluates Visual, Auditory, Reading/Writing, and Kinesthetic information processing preferences.
- **[Temperament & Cognitive Profiles](https://www.tester.lt/en/tests/temperament-types/):** Frameworks evaluating natural behavioral tendencies and cognitive styles.

### 2. Decision-Making & Life Audits (with AI Analysis)
- **[Cartesian Coordinates (Descartes Square) Audit](https://www.tester.lt/en/audits/descartes-square/):** A structured 4-question cognitive decision-making framework analyzing the consequences of action vs. inaction, enhanced with AI synthesis.
- **[Life Balance Audit (Wheel of Life)](https://www.tester.lt/en/audits/life-balance-wheel/):** Multi-dimensional evaluation across career, health, relationships, and personal fulfillment with AI-driven prioritization insights.

### 3. Career & Workplace Dynamics
- **[Holland Code (RIASEC) Career Test](https://www.tester.lt/en/tests/career-holland/):** Evaluates Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising, and Conventional career interests to match professional environments.
- **[Impostor Syndrome Assessment](https://www.tester.lt/en/tests/imposter-syndrome/):** Measures self-doubt patterns and competence attribution in high-achieving environments.
- **[Leadership & Workplace Audits](https://www.tester.lt/en/tests/leadership-style/):** Self-reflection tools for leadership style and salary negotiation readiness.

### 4. Mental Health & Emotional Well-Being
- **[Adult ADHD Self-Report Scale (ASRS)](https://www.tester.lt/en/tests/adhd-adults/):** Structured screening for attention, focus, and executive function patterns in adults.
- **[Job Burnout & Stress Audit](https://www.tester.lt/en/tests/emotional-burnout/):** Evaluates emotional exhaustion, depersonalization, and workplace recovery needs.
- **[Emotional Intelligence (EQ) Test](https://www.tester.lt/en/tests/emotional-intelligence/):** Assessments measuring empathy, self-regulation, and interpersonal boundary setting.

### 5. Relationships & Social Dynamics
- **[Attachment Styles Assessment](https://www.tester.lt/en/tests/attachment-styles/):** Explores Secure, Anxious, Avoidant, and Fearful-Avoidant patterns in close relationships.
- **[Conflict Resolution Styles](https://www.tester.lt/en/tests/conflict-styles/):** Identifies default behavioral responses during interpersonal disagreements.
- **[Love Languages Test](https://www.tester.lt/en/tests/love-languages/):** Evaluates how individuals prefer to give and receive affection.
